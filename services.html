Regular Cleaning

Upkeep for a home that is already in good order.

Includes

  • Dusting of accessible surfaces
  • Dusting furniture and decor
  • Vacuuming carpets and rugs
  • Sweeping and mopping floors
  • Cleaning and sanitizing bathrooms
  • Cleaning toilets, sinks, counters and mirrors
  • Cleaning kitchen countertops and backsplash
  • Cleaning exterior of kitchen appliances
  • Cleaning sinks and faucets
  • Dusting baseboards
  • Emptying garbage and replacing liners
  • General tidying of the areas being cleaned
  • High-touch surface cleaning

Not included: heavy buildup, detailed scrubbing, or the insides of appliances and cabinets. Those are a Deep Cleaning or an add-on.

Deep Cleaning

For a home that needs extra attention, or has not had a professional clean in a while.

Everything in Regular, plus

  • Detailed baseboard cleaning
  • Detailed door and door-frame cleaning
  • Cleaning trim and ledges
  • Detailed dusting of corners and hard-to-reach areas
  • Detailed bathroom scrubbing
  • Removal of soap scum and buildup
  • Detailed kitchen cleaning
  • Cleaning cabinet exteriors
  • Cleaning around appliances
  • Detailed floor cleaning
  • Removal of accumulated dust and dirt
  • Extra attention to buildup and neglected areas

Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning

For an empty place that has to be clean to the last corner, ready for a new occupant or buyer.

Everything in Deep, plus

  • Detailed cleaning of empty rooms
  • Detailed baseboards, trim, doors and frames
  • Detailed kitchen and bathroom cleaning
  • Cleaning inside closets
  • Cleaning shelves and accessible storage
  • Detailed floor cleaning
  • Dust removal from surfaces and fixtures
  • Cleaning light switches and high-touch areas
  • Cleaning window sills and tracks
  • Detailed cleaning of corners and hard-to-reach areas
  • Final detail inspection and finishing touches

Post-Renovation Cleaning

For the fine dust and residue the trades leave behind.

Includes

  • Removal of fine construction dust
  • Detailed dusting of walls and accessible surfaces
  • Dusting doors, frames, trim and baseboards
  • Cleaning window sills and accessible tracks
  • Cleaning cabinets and countertops
  • Detailed kitchen and bathroom cleaning
  • Vacuuming and mopping floors
  • Removal of fine dust from corners and hard-to-reach areas
  • Cleaning fixtures and accessible surfaces
  • Removal of light construction residue
  • Final detailed clean and finishing touches

Takes longer: heavy construction debris, paint splatter, drywall or mud residue, adhesive, grout or cement, stickers, or a lot of window cleaning. Mention it and Tiffany will quote for it.

Add-ons

Extras you can add to any clean. Tick them on the quote form, or just ask.

You can add

Two of these ask how many: windows and appliances.

  • Inside oven
  • Inside refrigerator
  • Interior windows
  • Inside cabinets and drawers
  • Wall washing
  • Inside kitchen appliances

Worth mentioning

Three things change how long a clean takes. The quote form asks about them, so Tiffany can quote it right the first time.

  • Heavy grime or buildup
  • More pet hair than usual
  • Heavy construction residue

Booking terms

Tiffany works across the City of Toronto — any postal code starting with M. Just outside the city, in the GTA? Ask; she sometimes travels for a fee agreed beforehand.

Deposit

No deposit is taken. This site sends Tiffany your details — it doesn't take payment and it doesn't book the job, so you arrange the day with her directly.

Cancellation

There is no cancellation charge. Call or text as early as you can and Tiffany will move the day with you.

Ready when you are

Tell Tiffany about your home — the clean, the size, any extras — and she comes back to you with a quote. About a minute, no account.
