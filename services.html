Four ways to get it squeaky clean

Priced by square footage, done by one set of hands. Here is exactly what is included in each visit.

Regular Cleaning

$0.25/ sq ft

Ideal for homes that are maintained on a regular basis.

Includes

  • Dusting of accessible surfaces
  • Dusting furniture and decor
  • Vacuuming carpets and rugs
  • Sweeping and mopping floors
  • Cleaning and sanitizing bathrooms
  • Cleaning toilets, sinks, counters and mirrors
  • Cleaning kitchen countertops and backsplash
  • Cleaning exterior of kitchen appliances
  • Cleaning sinks and faucets
  • Dusting baseboards
  • Emptying garbage and replacing liners
  • General tidying of the areas being cleaned
  • High-touch surface cleaning

Not included: Regular cleaning does not include heavy buildup, detailed scrubbing, or inside appliances and cabinets.

Deep Cleaning

$0.40/ sq ft

A more detailed clean for homes that need extra attention or have not been professionally cleaned recently.

Everything in Regular Cleaning, plus

  • Detailed baseboard cleaning
  • Detailed door and door-frame cleaning
  • Cleaning trim and ledges
  • Detailed dusting of corners and hard-to-reach areas
  • Detailed bathroom scrubbing
  • Removal of soap scum and buildup
  • Detailed kitchen cleaning
  • Cleaning cabinet exteriors
  • Cleaning around appliances
  • Detailed floor cleaning
  • Removal of accumulated dust and dirt
  • Extra attention to buildup and neglected areas

Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning

$0.65/ sq ft

Designed to leave a property exceptionally clean and ready for a new occupant or buyer.

Everything in Deep Cleaning, plus

  • Detailed cleaning of empty rooms
  • Detailed baseboards, trim, doors and frames
  • Detailed kitchen and bathroom cleaning
  • Cleaning inside closets
  • Cleaning shelves and accessible storage
  • Detailed floor cleaning
  • Dust removal from surfaces and fixtures
  • Cleaning light switches and high-touch areas
  • Cleaning window sills and tracks
  • Detailed cleaning of corners and hard-to-reach areas
  • Final detail inspection and finishing touches

Post-Renovation Cleaning

$0.75/ sq ft

Designed to remove fine construction dust and residue left behind after renovations.

Includes

  • Removal of fine construction dust
  • Detailed dusting of walls and accessible surfaces
  • Dusting doors, frames, trim and baseboards
  • Cleaning window sills and accessible tracks
  • Cleaning cabinets and countertops
  • Detailed kitchen and bathroom cleaning
  • Vacuuming and mopping floors
  • Removal of fine dust from corners and hard-to-reach areas
  • Cleaning fixtures and accessible surfaces
  • Removal of light construction residue
  • Final detailed clean and finishing touches

Note: additional charges may apply for heavy construction debris, paint splatter, drywall or mud residue, adhesive, grout or cement residue, stickers, or extensive window cleaning.
