Policies

The terms that keep a job fair for everyone, laid out plainly.

Deposit

Once online booking launches, a 50% deposit will be required to hold a full-day service: Deep Cleaning, Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning, or Post-Renovation Cleaning. Today there is no online booking and no payment is taken through this site: you get your price here, then arrange the day with Tiffany directly.

Cancellation

Cancel or move a booked job at least 48 hours before the start time and there is no charge. Inside 48 hours a 50% cancellation fee applies, because a full day held for you is a day that cannot be filled at short notice. These terms take effect with online booking; until then, just call or text as early as you can.

Service area

We serve the City of Toronto, meaning every M postal code. Inner and outer GTA are available for a travel fee ($35 inner GTA, $60 outer GTA).

What's outside a standard clean

A Regular Cleaning does not include heavy buildup, detailed scrubbing, or inside appliances and cabinets. Those are covered by Deep Cleaning or the add-ons below.

  • Inside oven, inside refrigerator, and interior windows (see pricing for rates)
  • Inside cabinets and drawers and wall washing start from $75, and inside kitchen appliances from $25 per appliance (see pricing)
  • Heavy grime, heavy pet hair, and heavy construction residue: quoted on request
