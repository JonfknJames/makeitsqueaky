How the price is worked out

  1. 1

    Rate × square footage

    Every service has a per-square-foot rate. Don't know your footage? The calculator estimates it from home type, bedrooms and bathrooms.

  2. 2

    Never below the minimum

    Small jobs are lifted to the $180 minimum rather than billed at the raw rate.

  3. 3

    Add-ons and travel on top

    Extras you choose are added next, then any travel fee — after the minimum, never absorbed by it.

Regular Cleaning

$0.25/ sq ft · half-day

Ideal for homes that are maintained on a regular basis.

Includes

  • Dusting of accessible surfaces
  • Dusting furniture and decor
  • Vacuuming carpets and rugs
  • Sweeping and mopping floors
  • Cleaning and sanitizing bathrooms
  • Cleaning toilets, sinks, counters and mirrors
  • Cleaning kitchen countertops and backsplash
  • Cleaning exterior of kitchen appliances
  • Cleaning sinks and faucets
  • Dusting baseboards
  • Emptying garbage and replacing liners
  • General tidying of the areas being cleaned
  • High-touch surface cleaning

Not included: Regular cleaning does not include heavy buildup, detailed scrubbing, or inside appliances and cabinets.

          Booking terms

          What applies today, and what changes when online booking opens.

          Deposit

          No deposit is taken today. This site gives you a price — it doesn't take payment and it doesn't book the job, so you arrange the day with Tiffany directly.

          When online booking opens, a 50% deposit will hold a full day: Deep Cleaning, Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning, or Post-Renovation Cleaning. A Regular Cleaning is a half day; whether it takes a deposit is still to be settled.

          Cancellation

          There is no cancellation charge today. Call or text as early as you can and Tiffany will move the day with you.

          When online booking opens, these terms take effect: cancel or move a booked job at least 48 hours before the start time at no charge; inside 48 hours a 50% fee applies to any booking, half day or full, because time held for you is time that cannot be filled at short notice.
