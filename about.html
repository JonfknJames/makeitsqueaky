About Make it Squeaky

Over 12 years of professional cleaning experience, and one set of hands on every job.

Hi, I'm Tiffany

I'm the owner of Make it Squeaky. With over 12 years of professional cleaning experience, I've built my career around providing reliable, detailed, high-quality cleaning. I've had the opportunity to work on everything from regular residential cleanings to deep cleans, move-in and move-out cleans, and post-renovation projects.

I started Make it Squeaky because I wanted to create a cleaning service where quality, trust and attention to detail come first. I believe a clean home should feel truly clean, not just look clean.

I take pride in the work I do, and I treat every home with the same care and respect I would want for my own. Whether you need regular maintenance or a detailed clean from top to bottom, my goal is to make your space feel fresh, comfortable and beautifully clean.

Thank you for considering Make it Squeaky. I look forward to making your space squeaky!
